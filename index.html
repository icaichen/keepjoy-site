Declutter smarter. Keep memories that truly matter.

KeepJoy is a mindful decluttering companion that helps you organize your home, track your progress, and document the stories behind your belongings. Clean with purpose, let go with clarity, and build a space you love living in.

Features

Smart Progress Dashboard

See your decluttering stats in real time — sessions, categories, outcomes, and where your time truly goes. Understand your habits and make steady progress.

Before–After Stories

Document every transformation. Compare spaces before and after you declutter and build a visual record of your growth.

Meaningful Memory Archive

Keep photos, notes, and sentimental stories behind the items you decide to keep or let go. A gentle way to honor your past while moving forward.

Personalized Declutter Modes

Choose guided modes based on your energy, time, and goals — from quick sessions to deeper emotional decluttering.
